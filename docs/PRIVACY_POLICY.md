# Privacy Policy for Showly+

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

Showly+ ("we," "our," or "the App") is an open-source TV show and movie tracking application. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Google Drive Data
Showly+ offers a cloud backup feature using the Google Drive API.
- **Scope**: The App requests access to the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata` scope.
- **Usage**: This permission is used exclusively to store and retrieve your backup files (watchlists, progress, and settings).
- **Storage**: All backup data is stored in your private **Google Drive App Data folder**. This folder is isolated from the rest of your Google Drive and is only accessible by Showly+. We do not have access to any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
- **Control**: You can disconnect your Google account or delete your backups at any time through the App's settings.

### b. Third-Party Services
The App integrates with several third-party services to provide its core functionality:
- **Trakt.tv**: If you sign in, your data is synced with your Trakt account.
- **TMDB / OMDB / Moctale**: Used to fetch metadata and ratings.

## 2. Data Security
Your data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your private Google Drive App Data folder. We do not host any personal data on our own servers.

## 3. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
