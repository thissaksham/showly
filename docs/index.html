Showly+

A modern TV Shows & Movies tracking app.

About Showly+

Showly+ is an open-source application designed to help you track your progress on TV shows and movies, synchronized with Trakt.tv.

Key Features

  • Track your watch progress effortlessly.
  • Discover trending and popular shows and movies.
  • Manage your personal collection and custom lists.
  • Sync with Trakt.tv for a seamless experience.
  • Securely backup your data to your private Google Drive App Data folder.

Showly+ is a tracking app, not a streaming service. It does not provide content for watching, streaming, or downloading.
